Our Story

From one Mississauga counter to four Ontario branches.

BitTrust was founded in 2018 to make buying and selling cryptocurrency simple, secure, and accessible. What started as a local service in Mississauga has grown into a trusted crypto and currency exchange with multiple locations across Ontario.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make crypto and currency exchange more accessible, secure, and trustworthy for customers in Toronto, Mississauga, Thornhill, and Hamilton.
