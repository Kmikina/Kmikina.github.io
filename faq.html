Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about verification, payments, limits and our locations.

What is BitTrust?

BitTrust is a Canadian cryptocurrency and currency exchange company offering secure in-person crypto exchange and foreign currency exchange services across Ontario.

Is BitTrust registered in Canada?

Yes. BitTrust Inc. is a Canadian-registered Money Services Business (MSB) registered with FINTRAC under registration number M21441278.

What services does BitTrust offer?

Cryptocurrency buying and selling, foreign currency exchange, OTC crypto transactions, cash crypto transactions, in-person crypto support, and wallet setup assistance for beginners.

What is KYC?

KYC ("Know Your Customer") refers to the identity verification process required by financial institutions and regulated businesses.

How does KYC verification work?

Customers must provide valid government-issued photo identification such as a passport, driver's licence, or provincial photo ID. Additional verification may be required depending on transaction amount and compliance requirements.

How long does ID verification take?

BitTrust uses an instant verification system, allowing most customers to be verified within minutes.

Can international visitors buy or sell cryptocurrency?

Yes. International visitors may complete transactions using valid government-issued identification such as a passport, subject to compliance review and transaction monitoring requirements.

How can I buy cryptocurrency?

Visit a BitTrust location in Toronto, Mississauga, Thornhill, or Hamilton where our staff will guide you through the process.

How can I sell cryptocurrency?

Bring your cryptocurrency to any BitTrust location. Once confirmed, payment is processed quickly and securely.

Which cryptocurrencies can I buy or sell?

BitTrust supports Bitcoin, Ethereum, Tether, Solana, XRP, Dogecoin, Shiba Inu, Cardano, BNB, Polygon, and many other popular digital assets.

What payment methods are accepted?

Cash, debit card, e-Transfer, and wire transfer (for eligible OTC transactions).

Can I receive cash for cryptocurrency?

Yes. Eligible customers can receive cash for cryptocurrency transactions completed at our locations.

How long does a cryptocurrency transaction take?

Most cryptocurrency transactions are completed instantly or within the same day after payment and verification are confirmed.

How much cryptocurrency can I buy or sell?

Buy cryptocurrency starting from $500 CAD to over $10,000 CAD. Sell cryptocurrency starting from $100 CAD to over $10,000 CAD.

Can I buy or sell large amounts?

Yes. BitTrust offers OTC services designed for large cryptocurrency transactions with personalized pricing, privacy, and dedicated support.

Does BitTrust hold customer funds or wallets?

No. BitTrust does not provide ongoing custodial services and does not hold customer wallets or private keys outside the execution of transactions.

Is BitTrust safe?

We prioritize security through secure verification procedures, compliance monitoring, and in-person support.

Can BitTrust help if I'm new to cryptocurrency?

Absolutely. Our team is happy to help beginners understand cryptocurrency, answer questions, and assist with setting up a secure crypto wallet.

Does BitTrust provide financial advice?

No. BitTrust does not provide financial, investment, legal, or tax advice.

Does BitTrust charge fees?

Yes. Transaction fees and exchange rates vary depending on transaction amount, payment method, and market conditions.

What foreign currencies are exchanged?

USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SAR, INR, PKR, CNY, JPY, KRW, and many additional global currencies.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome at most locations, though advance contact is recommended for large transactions.

Which locations are available?

Toronto, Mississauga, Thornhill, and Hamilton across Ontario.

Are cryptocurrency transactions reversible?

No. Cryptocurrency transactions conducted on blockchain networks are generally irreversible once confirmed.

What risks should I know?

Cryptocurrency markets are highly volatile and prices may change rapidly. Customers should understand the risks associated with digital assets before completing any transaction.

How can I contact BitTrust?

Reach out by phone, email, or visit one of our Ontario locations.
