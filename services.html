Services FAQ

Good questions.

What services does BitTrust offer?

Cryptocurrency buying and selling, foreign currency exchange, OTC crypto transactions, cash crypto transactions, in-person crypto support, and wallet setup assistance for beginners.

How much cryptocurrency can I buy or sell?

Buy cryptocurrency starting from $500 CAD to over $10,000 CAD. Sell cryptocurrency starting from $100 CAD to over $10,000 CAD.

Can I buy or sell large amounts?

Yes. BitTrust offers OTC services designed for large cryptocurrency transactions with personalized pricing, privacy, and dedicated support.

Does BitTrust hold customer funds or wallets?

No. BitTrust does not provide ongoing custodial services and does not hold customer wallets or private keys outside the execution of transactions.
